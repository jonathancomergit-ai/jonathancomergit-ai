### My big three

**Timeless** — What if your health bar was a clock? You don't lose hit points, you lose **TIME**, and when it runs out so do you. So playing it safe is actually how you die! Custom engine, custom room editor, 43,000 lines, and three bosses who each break a rule the game just spent an hour teaching you.
→ [Read more about it](https://jonjoe1001.dev/projects/timeless.html)  ·  *coming to Steam*

**Emergent Evolver** — Ever been curious about machine learning, anatomy, or evolution? You draw the body, and that's it — you never tell it how to move! The brain starts as pure noise and grows its own neurons while it learns. Give it a few hundred generations and the thing **RUNS**. Best part? You watch the brain wire itself up live while it works it out.
→ [Play it in your browser](https://jonjoe1001.itch.io)  ·  [more about it](https://jonjoe1001.dev/projects/emergent-evolver.html)

**Wheelhouse** — OK, we all have games but no games, am I right? Game night, solved. It reads what your Steam Family *actually* owns and only puts games on the wheel that everybody can launch. No more landing on something one person bought two years ago.
→ [More about it](https://jonjoe1001.dev/projects/wheelhouse.html)
